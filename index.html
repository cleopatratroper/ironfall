Playing with friends

01

One of you creates a group

Hit CREATE A GROUP on the title screen. You get a four-character code and become the host — your machine runs the bots, the loot and the gas for everyone.

02

Everyone else types the code

JOIN WITH A CODE, four characters, enter. You'll appear in the host's lobby and see who else is in. Up to eight humans; bots fill the rest of the forty.

03

The host starts the match

Everyone picks their own landing zone off the same map. Group members show up in blue with a nameplate and a health bar — and yes, they can shoot you. It's a free-for-all.

04

No servers involved

Connections are direct browser-to-browser over WebRTC. Nothing is installed, nothing is stored, and no game traffic touches a server — only the initial handshake does.

Group play needs this page to be served over the web, so it won't connect from a file:// copy or inside a preview pane. The host has to keep their tab open for the match to continue.

How a match plays out

01

Pick your drop

The island map opens first. Each compound shows how many rivals are heading there — the busy ones hold the best loot and the shortest life expectancy.

02

Win the loot race

Everyone lands empty-handed, you included. Weapons come in four rarities; plates give you a second health bar. The first thirty seconds decide a lot.

03

Rotate ahead of the gas

The circle closes in seven phases, each faster and more painful than the last. Late phases take twenty health a second — being caught out is fatal, not inconvenient.

04

Outlast the other 39

Your rivals fight each other whether or not you pull a trigger, so the lobby thins on its own. Surviving is a perfectly valid strategy right up until it isn't.

The arsenal

Six weapons

Sidearm P9, Vector-9 SMG, FR-556 rifle, Bulwark LMG, Breach-12 shotgun and the Longshot sniper — each with its own recoil, spread, range falloff and ammo type.

Four rarities

Rarity raises damage and magazine size, so a legendary sidearm can beat a common rifle. Check the colour before you commit.

COMMON RARE EPIC LEGENDARY

Staying alive

Plates absorb damage before health does. Bandages patch you to 75; medkits take longer but fill you completely. Headshots hurt — up to 2.4× on the sniper.

Built with

A hand-written engine

No game libraries. The renderer is raw WebGL with its own shaders, matrix maths and mesh builder — roughly 71,000 triangles of island, batched into a handful of draw calls.

A generated island

The terrain is value-noise fractal heightmap with an island falloff, flattened under each compound so the buildings sit level. Nine named locations, 26 outlying huts, 900 trees.

39 opponents

Each rival loots, rotates ahead of the circle, takes cover behind line-of-sight checks, heals when hurt and fights the others. A full match resolves in about five minutes.

Host-authoritative netcode

The host simulates the world and ships snapshots at 14Hz; clients predict their own movement and interpolate everything else. The island is generated from a shared seed, so nobody has to send the map.
